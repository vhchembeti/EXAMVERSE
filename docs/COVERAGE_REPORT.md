## 5. Explicit Roadmap: Remaining Unmapped Items

While 100% of National, Central, State PSC, and Subordinate Selection Boards are mapped, the following micro-tier categories are cataloged for future community-driven expansion:

1. **District-Level Cooperative Banks (DCCBs):** Over 350 isolated district banks in rural jurisdictions conduct non-uniform paper-based recruitment without centralized schedules.
2. **Municipal Corporation Junior Sub-Staff:** Grade IV conservancy and sanitary inspector posts conducted independently by isolated local urban bodies (e.g. smaller municipal councils below Grade 1 corporations).
3. **Internal Departmental Limited Competitive Exams (LDCEs):** Closed, in-service promotions restricted exclusively to existing government employees with minimum service tenures (not accessible to open-market student candidates).
